# Rebuild

A 16-week return-to-gym program after birth, built as an installable phone app.

**Open the app:** https://tomule1urbule.github.io/rebuild/

## Install it on your phone

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari → tap **Share** → **Add to Home Screen**.
- **Android:** open the link in Chrome → tap **⋮** → **Install app**.

It then opens full screen from your home screen and works offline at the gym. Your logged workouts are saved on your phone.

## What's inside

- Four phases (Reconnect, Rebuild, Strengthen, Return), three gym sessions a week
- Tailored to your birth type (vaginal or C-section) and training background
- 37 exercises with animated demos, technique guides and step-by-step cues
- Daily check-in that lightens the session on tired days, workout log, and move-on checks
- Short reads on the pelvic floor, tummy gap, stop signs, feeding and returning to impact

*General guidance, not medical advice. Check with your GP or a pelvic health physiotherapist before you start.*
